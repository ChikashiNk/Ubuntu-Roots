About Ubuntu Roots

Rooted in the spirit of Ubuntu — community and connection — our project bridges learners world wide with global volunteers through digital education. We offer online lessons, workshops, and mentorship, focusing on literacy, hygiene, finance, and other essential life skills.

We believe education is a right for all. By creating a supportive, inspiring learning space, we empower children to grow, dream, and thrive. Together, we live the Ubuntu philosophy: “I am because we are.”
